This is just a mini website to give a brief biograghy about personality My name is Jeremiah Sahr Komba, but many know me as Dignitary Jeremiah 🖤. I’ve always had a deep passion for creativity, technology, and building things—not just physically, but digitally too. That passion has grown with me, shaping who I am today. I’m currently studying civil engineering, a field that allows me to express my love for structure and design. I enjoy making construction plans, drawing architectural sketches, and visualizing buildings even before they exist. There’s a special kind of satisfaction that comes with turning an idea into something real. Alongside engineering, I found a second passion in web development. I began learning how to create websites using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and I haven’t stopped since. To me, web development is more than code—it’s a way to connect people, share knowledge, and make ideas accessible. One of my proudest projects is BeccaLearn, a digital platform I created to help students access books, past papers, quizzes, and study materials from anywhere.
